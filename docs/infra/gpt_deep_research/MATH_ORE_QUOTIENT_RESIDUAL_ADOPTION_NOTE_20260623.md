## Local Adoption

Adopted:

- The strongest abstract problem is an object-existence question in a finite
  weighted product space:

```text
Given X = product_j A_j, weights w, a pre-outcome nuisance subspace N,
and observed fields K_t, does R_t = Pi_{N_perp,w} K_t survive as a stable
non-scalar object?
```

- A valid positive answer must show that the residual is above noise, not a
  rank-1 scalar shadow, better than random same-dimension subspaces and random
  partitions, and stable under preregistered coarsening/refinement.
- A valid negative answer is valuable: it can show that apparent collapse
  complexity reduces to scalar projection, low-rank templates, nuisance, or
  coordinate freedom.
- For the current MaoField branch, the concrete compatible objects remain:
  `r_i = u_i - <v,u_i>_w v` for future q4 full-panel analysis, and
  `I_i = Pi_{A_perp,w} K_i` for the `Q_freq4 x B_tokenpos4` interaction
  candidate.
- Philosophical terms are allowed only after operational translation:
  contradiction = non-additive interaction / non-commuting operation / gluing
  obstruction; essence = quotient residual; mediation = outcome-independent
  axis or projection; totality = fixed product space, weights, nuisance, and
  negative controls; motion = generation path in quotient or tensor space.

Deflated:

- Existing smoke data do not establish a stable non-scalar structure. They only
  reproduce a small `smoke_conjecture_only` interaction residual over three
  smoke checkpoints.
- Any report-side `local_test.json` or local reproduction is not a canonical
  MaoField evidence source; numerical trust must come from existing canonical
  smoke artifacts and future provenance-checked panels.
- The low uncentered `sigma2/sigma1` in the smoke audit makes the safest default
  interpretation a near-single-template residual, not multi-directional
  dynamics.
- `I != 0` proves only non-additivity after the chosen projection on the chosen
  cell system. It does not prove mechanism, philosophical structure, model
  interpretability, or glass-box access.
- Sheaf/gluing, commutator, tensor-rank, and dynamic-path language is future
  mathematical structure unless tied to explicit artifacts and nulls.

Rejected / blocked:

- No full panel has run.
- No 16-cell full-panel aggregate exists.
- No hypercube interaction field has been observed.
- No residual field has been observed.
- No `LOSO passed`, `F3 positive`, `glass box broken`, training authorization,
  or new loss authorization is supported.
- Philosophical language cannot bridge an evidentiary gap.
