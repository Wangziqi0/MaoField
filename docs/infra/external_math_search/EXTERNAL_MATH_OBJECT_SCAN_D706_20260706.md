### 1. Gauge-Quotiented Finite Consistency Radius

Status:

```text
BUILD_NOW
```

This is the safest direct landing of Report27. It is essentially:

```text
Delta^2_{12}(s_1,s_2; Gamma_12)
  = min_{gamma in Gamma_12} ||rho_1 s_1 - rho_2 s_2 - gamma||^2_w.
```

It refines the Robinson consistency-radius idea by adding a declared
finite-dimensional gauge/nuisance subspace and by specializing to residual
metric audits with exact rational controls.

Safe claim:

```text

gauge-quotiented certificate for whether two local residual-like objects can be
pasted modulo declared nuisance.
```

Unsafe claim:

```text

contextuality framework.
```
