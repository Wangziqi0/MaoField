## Eligible requests

Access may be considered for journal editors and reviewers and for researchers with a verifiable research identity who seek to audit or reproduce claims in the associated Article. A request must state the requester's name, affiliation or research identity, purpose, requested materials, intended analyses, security arrangements and requested retention period.

## Decision basis

Requests are assessed for scientific relevance, proportionality, information-security safeguards, provider redistribution rights and risk of exposing reusable hidden answers. The operator may approve, deny or ask for clarification. Denial or narrowing may be necessary when provider terms, confidentiality, security or evaluation integrity prevent disclosure.

## Data-use conditions

Approved access is limited to the stated purpose and named recipients. Recipients must not redistribute the materials, disclose hidden answers, attempt to obtain credentials or private keys, use the materials to compromise future evaluations, or publish provider-restricted content. Derived public outputs must respect the Article's frozen claim ceiling and applicable licences. Access may require acceptance of a data-use agreement.

## Security, retention and deletion

Recipients must use access-controlled encrypted storage, minimize local copies and report suspected disclosure promptly. Unless a shorter period is specified in the approval, controlled materials must be deleted within 90 days after the approved project ends or access expires, and deletion must be confirmed on request. The operator may revoke access for breach of these conditions.

## Exclusions

API credentials, DPAPI/private/decryption keys and personal secrets are never provided. Provider records lacking redistribution permission and reusable hidden-answer material remain unavailable unless a specific, lawful and secure reviewer-access route permits them.

This policy defines the process; it does not claim that a public or reviewer archive already exists. A durable access endpoint will be created and tested before journal submission.
