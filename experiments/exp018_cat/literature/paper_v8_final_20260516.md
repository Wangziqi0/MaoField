## Abstract

**Background.** Self-iteration model collapse in language models, as documented by Shumailov et al. (2024, *Nature*), describes perplexity degradation when models are recursively trained on their own generations. Borji (2024) surfaces an unexplained empirical anomaly: Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence stabilizes within a finite range across iterations while Wasserstein distance continuously grows — a phenomenon for which the existing model-collapse framing offers no quantitative mechanism. Dohmatob (2025) provides a Markov-chain-level $\zeta$-extra-term analysis specific to linear regression, but does not explain the KL stabilization observed in language modeling.

**Approach.** We conduct an **empirical pilot study** (single-architecture OPT-125M + single-dataset WikiText-2 + single-paradigm SFT-only + N=4 multi-seed) of a **two-term EMA-deviation contradiction loss form** that emerged from code implementation and was retrospectively recognized as admitting a dialectical-structural mapping:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\rm cont}^{\rm chain,\,actual}(\theta_n) = (\Delta D_n)^2 + (D_n - \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n)^2$$
where $D_n$ is the train-signal KL divergence between an exponential-moving-average (EMA) teacher model and the current student model, computed on a fixed validation batch every $\tau = 10$ training steps (the `kl_update_every` chain config parameter). The **current form emerged from code implementation prior to mathematical derivation**; the resulting mathematical structure was retrospectively recognized to admit a structural mapping with classical dialectical materialism principles (Mao 1937 *On Contradiction* §3 internal-external dialectical mapping; see §7). We report multi-seed N=4 paired-t analysis on OPT-125M trained on Wikitext-2, framework prediction-observation null-consistency honest disclosure under the dimensionally-clean v8 mean-field NESS form (Reading 2, see §3.6.2), and a binary catalog of alternative families satisfying LLM-domain constraints. Multi-seed Phase 1 chain experiments (N=4, seeds 1-4 on OPT-125M with WikiText-2, 10 generations each) were run with the chain config $\lambda_1 = \lambda_2 = \lambda_3 = 1.0$ (uniform weights), $\beta_{\rm kl} = 0.9$, $\beta_\theta = 0.999$, $K=1$ (effectively two-term, $T_2$ third-difference term is zero due to $K=1$ history buffer), $\tau = 10$ (KL update every 10 training steps). We make no claim of axiom-first derivation, no claim of universal mitigation, no claim of substantive prediction success, and no claim of systematic multi-architecture study.

## §1 Introduction

### §1.1 Empirical anomaly that motivates the study

Borji 2024 (arXiv 2410.12954) in critiquing Shumailov 2024 *Nature* *AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated data* surfaces an empirical anomaly not mechanistically explained by existing frameworks:

> "KL divergence increases initially then **stabilizes within a range**, while Wasserstein distance continuously grows across iterations." (Borji 2024)

This phenomenon is visible in Shumailov 2024 §5.2 own experimental data (OPT-125M wikitext-2 5 epochs no_preserve baseline) — perplexity at generation 5-9 forms a plateau, not divergent to infinity. But Shumailov 2024 main framing is "model collapse Markov absorbing state irreversible", with no explanation for why empirical KL stabilizes within a finite range.

Dohmatob 2025 ICLR *Strong Model Collapse* provides Markov-chain-level $\zeta$-extra-term mathematical quantification, explaining sample size scaling does not mitigate, but the $\zeta$-extra-term is derived in linear regression specific case and does not explain the KL stabilization phenomenon.

Ferbach 2024 sophisticated iterative mixing / Yang 2025 synthetic data verification / Sahiti 2024 mitigation works all stay at engineering layer mitigation and do not give KL stabilization mechanism.

**Open empirical question**: Why does KL divergence stabilize within a finite range across iterations rather than diverging to infinity or collapsing to zero? What determines this stable range? How can its quantitative bound be derived?

## §3 Chain actual two-term contradiction loss form

### §3.1 ℒ_矛盾 chain actual form (v6 保留)

Let $D_n \in [0, +\infty)$ be the train-signal KL divergence at generation $n$ between the EMA teacher model and the current student model on a fixed validation batch (256 wikitext-2 val sentences, 64 tokens, batch=8 in `compute_kl`, every 10 train steps):

$$D_n^{\rm code} := \mathrm{KL}(q^{\rm EMA}_n \| p^{\theta_n}_n) = \frac{1}{\sum_{b,t} m_{b,t}}\,\sum_{b,t}\,m_{b,t}\,\sum_{v}\,q^{\rm EMA}_{n,b,t}(v)\,\bigl(\log q^{\rm EMA}_{n,b,t}(v) - \log p^{\theta_n}_{n,b,t}(v)\bigr)$$

where $q^{\rm EMA}_n = \mathrm{softmax}(\mathrm{logits}_{\bar\theta_n})$ is the EMA model output (fully detached), $p^{\theta_n}_n = \mathrm{softmax}(\mathrm{logits}_{\theta_n})$ is the current model output (with grad on $\theta_n$), $b, t$ index batch/token positions, $m_{b,t}$ is attention mask (padding excluded), $v$ indexes vocab (50257 for OPT-125M).

The chain actual contradiction loss form (`contradiction_loss.py` line 213-217 with chain config `cat_arm_b.yaml` overrides $\lambda_i = 1$, $T_2$ form `"relu_dpp"`, $K=1$):

$$\boxed{\;\mathcal{L}_{\rm cont}^{\rm chain,\,actual}(\theta_n) = (\Delta D_n)^2 + (D_n - \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n)^2\;}$$

where:
- $\Delta D_n := D_n - D_{n-1}$ (one-step forward difference, $D_{n-1}$ fully detached)
- $\bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n := \beta_{\rm kl} \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_{n-1} + (1-\beta_{\rm kl}) D_{n-1}$ (EMA recursive update, fully detached on $\theta_n$)
- $\beta_{\rm kl} = 0.9$ (chain config, **yaml-independent parameter directly chosen as yaml override**, **not derived from any $m_{\rm eff}$ exponentiation relation**; v6 P0-4 honest disclose: earlier v5 §3.1 line 187 wrote `β_kl = e^{-0.105}` which would imply $m_{\rm eff} = 0.105$, but this is mathematically inconsistent with the chain config $m_{\rm eff} = 1.0$ — where $e^{-1.0} \approx 0.368 \neq 0.9$ — and is retracted; see §3.2 + appendix E three-way reconcile for chain config decomposition; the relation between $\beta_{\rm kl}$ and $m_{\rm eff}$ via $\beta_{\rm kl} = e^{-m_{\rm eff} \cdot \tau_{\rm horizon}}$ for some implicit time-horizon $\tau_{\rm horizon}$ is a post-hoc semantic interpretation, not a chain config derivation; reconciliation with the canonical default is deferred to F-1 Phase 2)

**Why two-term, not three-term**: The `T_2_form` chain config is `"relu_dpp"` with the implementation
$$T_2 = \mathrm{ReLU}(D_n - 2 D_{n-1} + D_{n-2})$$
which requires $|D_{\rm history}| \geq 2$. The chain config `kl_history_K = 1` (yaml default missing, schema falls to `getattr(yaml_cat, "kl_history_K", 1) = 1`) limits the history buffer to at most one previous element. Consequently the `if len(D_history) == 1` branch fires and sets `D_doubleprime = torch.zeros_like(D_n)`, yielding $T_2 = \mathrm{ReLU}(0) = 0$ for all generations $n \geq 1$. **The chain therefore runs effectively a two-term loss**, with the velocity term $T_1 = (\Delta D_n)^2$ and the EMA-deviation term $T_3 = (D_n - \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n)^2$ as the only two terms entering loss and contributing to gradient.

**Coefficient values**: $\lambda_1 = \lambda_3 = 1$ (chain config YAML overrides). The Klein-Gordon-style coefficients $\lambda_1 = 1/(2 m_{\rm eff}), \lambda_2 = m_{\rm eff}/2, \lambda_3 = m_{\rm eff}$ are specified in `cat_arm_b_v2_dialectical.yaml` but **that config was never launched in the chain** (`phase1_robust_chain.sh` line 75 hardcodes `--config configs/cat_arm_b.yaml`).

#### §3.6.4 Banach contraction Lipschitz (chain actual form, v6 preserved)

The Banach map $T(D) = D - 4\eta\alpha(D - D^*) - \eta J_S^{\rm per-step}$ (per-step, before $\tau$ aggregation) has Lipschitz constant:

$$|T(D_1) - T(D_2)| = |D_1 - D_2| \cdot |1 - 4\eta\alpha|$$

Contraction $\Leftrightarrow |1 - 4\eta\alpha| < 1 \Leftrightarrow 0 < \eta\alpha < 1/2$.

Chain config values: $\eta = 2 \times 10^{-5}$ (AdamW learning rate), $\alpha = 10$. Then $\eta\alpha = 2 \times 10^{-4} \ll 1/2$, so:

$$\rho^{\rm code,\,per-train-step} = 1 - 4\eta\alpha = 1 - 8 \times 10^{-4} = 0.99920$$

Per-train-step half-convergence: $n_{1/2}^{\rm train-step} = \log 0.5 / \log 0.99920 = 866$ train steps $\approx 0.6$ epoch (chain 1460 steps/epoch).

**Per-generation compound**: Single generation = 1460 train steps. Contradiction loss compute every 10 train steps, giving 146 contradiction-loss-active updates per generation. Per-generation Banach contraction ratio:

$$\rho^{\rm code,\,per-gen} = (1 - 4\eta\alpha)^{N_{\rm contr}} = 0.99920^{146} = 0.890$$

Per-generation half-convergence: $n_{1/2}^{\rm gen} = \log 0.5 / \log 0.890 = 5.94$ generations.
