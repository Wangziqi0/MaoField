## Abstract

**Background.** Self-iteration model collapse in language models, as documented by Shumailov et al. (2024, *Nature*), describes perplexity degradation when models are recursively trained on their own generations. Borji (2024) surfaces an unexplained empirical anomaly: Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence stabilizes within a finite range across iterations while Wasserstein distance continuously grows — a phenomenon for which the existing model-collapse framing offers no quantitative mechanism. Dohmatob (2025) provides a Markov-chain-level $\zeta$-extra-term analysis specific to linear regression, but does not explain the KL stabilization observed in language modeling.

**Approach.** This paper documents an empirical study of a **two-term EMA-deviation contradiction loss** for self-iteration training regularization:
$$\mathcal{L}_{\rm cont}^{\rm chain,\,actual}(\theta_n) = (\Delta D_n)^2 + (D_n - \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n)^2$$
where $D_n$ is the train-signal KL divergence between an exponential-moving-average (EMA) teacher model and the current student model, computed on a fixed validation batch. The current form **emerged from code implementation prior to mathematical derivation**; the resulting mathematical structure was retrospectively recognized to admit a structural mapping with classical dialectical materialism principles (Mao 1937 *On Contradiction* §3 internal-external dialectical mapping; see §7). We make no claim of axiom-first derivation; the contradiction loss is one specific instantiation in a broader constraint-driven family (see §3.3). Multi-seed Phase 1 chain experiments (N=4, seeds 1-4 on OPT-125M with WikiText-2, 10 generations each) were run with the chain config $\lambda_1 = \lambda_2 = 1.0$ (uniform weights), $\beta_{\rm kl} = 0.9$, $\beta_\theta = 0.999$, $K=1$ (effectively two-term, T_2 third-difference term is zero due to $K=1$ history buffer).

**Findings.**
- **Partial D4 shape robustness 5/5 PASS (STRONG ROBUST)** across both $\alpha = 0$ baseline and $\alpha = 10$ framework chains: U-shape pattern + low gen-0 baseline CV (0.22%) + spike + plateau + sliding-window consistency. This robustness is the model-collapse phenomenon's reproducibility, **not** evidence of framework $\alpha$-regularization effect.
- **F3 framework α=10 plateau effect NOT substantiated**: N=4 paired test-perplexity (α=10 vs α=0 plateau gen 6-9 mean) gives mean $-0.57\%$ ($p_{\rm two} = 0.82$). Sample size for paired-t (df=3) is small; "F3 NOT substantiated" does not equal "framework effect truly absent", but is an honest report of framework predictive carrier failure on this experiment.
- **+29% absolute level discrepancy**: A mean-field NESS Banach contraction analysis on the chain actual two-term form predicts stationary perplexity ${\rm PPL}_\infty = D^* - J_S/(4\alpha) \approx 43.4$ at $\alpha=10$, but measured value is $56.1 \pm 2.4$ ($z \approx 4.3\sigma$). Four candidate explanations honestly disclosed: (a) finite-N transient not yet at NESS; (b) higher-order corrections beyond mean-field linearization; (c) test set $q_*$ vs training-induced limit mismatch; (d) **train-signal $D_n^{\rm code}$ (KL between EMA and current model on validation set) versus paper-defined predictive $D_n^{\rm paper}$ (log-perplexity-ratio on test set) are mathematically distinct random variables; their stationary equality is an open substantive question** (newly identified in v4 by code-first audit).

**Future work.** Three substantive mathematical gaps relating chain actual form to Klein-Gordon-Hartree-Volterra extensions: (i) Klein-Gordon coefficient form-borrowed Lagrangian implementation (currently $\lambda_i = 1$ uniform, Klein-Gordon-style $\lambda_1 = 1/(2 m_{\rm eff}), \lambda_2 = m_{\rm eff}/2, \lambda_3 = m_{\rm eff}$ is post-hoc recognition not chain implementation); (ii) Volterra $K=9$ third-difference term lift (currently $K=1$ chain config makes T_2 term zero in loss); (iii) cross-generation chain-rule with gradient flow through history (currently history detached, no cross-generation gradient). Multi-architecture (Llama, Pythia) and multi-seed N $\geq$ 8 paired-test extensions are deferred 1-2 months and 2-3 weeks respectively. Closure of the +29% absolute level discrepancy is deferred to multi-architecture $H(q_*)$ empirical fit and resolution of the $D^{\rm code}$ vs $D^{\rm paper}$ definition mismatch.

## §1 Introduction

### §1.1 Empirical anomaly that motivates the study

Borji 2024 (arXiv 2410.12954) in critiquing Shumailov 2024 *Nature* *AI models collapse when trained on recursively generated data* surfaces an empirical anomaly not mechanistically explained by existing frameworks:

> "KL divergence increases initially then **stabilizes within a range**, while Wasserstein distance continuously grows across iterations." (Borji 2024)

This phenomenon is visible in Shumailov 2024 §5.2 own experimental data (OPT-125M wikitext-2 5 epochs no_preserve baseline) — perplexity at generation 5-9 forms a plateau, not divergent to infinity. But Shumailov 2024 main framing is "model collapse Markov absorbing state irreversible", with no explanation for why empirical KL stabilizes within a finite range.

Dohmatob 2025 ICLR *Strong Model Collapse* provides Markov-chain-level $\zeta$-extra-term mathematical quantification, explaining sample size scaling does not mitigate, but the $\zeta$-extra-term is derived in linear regression specific case and does not explain the KL stabilization phenomenon.

Ferbach 2024 sophisticated iterative mixing / Yang 2025 synthetic data verification / Sahiti 2024 mitigation works all stay at engineering layer mitigation and do not give KL stabilization mechanism.

**Open empirical question**: Why does KL divergence stabilize within a finite range across iterations rather than diverging to infinity or collapsing to zero? What determines this stable range? How can its quantitative bound be derived?

## §3 Chain actual two-term contradiction loss form

### §3.1 ℒ_矛盾 chain actual form (v4 重写,推翻 v3 three-term Klein-Gordon Hartree-Volterra)

**v4 关键 substantive 重构(纪律 3 改 paper 追代码)**:

Let $D_n \in [0, +\infty)$ be the train-signal KL divergence at generation $n$ between the EMA teacher model and the current student model on a fixed validation batch (256 wikitext-2 val sentences, 64 tokens, batch=8 in `compute_kl`, every 10 train steps):

$$D_n^{\rm code} := \mathrm{KL}(q^{\rm EMA}_n \| p^{\theta_n}_n) = \frac{1}{\sum_{b,t} m_{b,t}}\,\sum_{b,t}\,m_{b,t}\,\sum_{v}\,q^{\rm EMA}_{n,b,t}(v)\,\bigl(\log q^{\rm EMA}_{n,b,t}(v) - \log p^{\theta_n}_{n,b,t}(v)\bigr)$$

where $q^{\rm EMA}_n = \mathrm{softmax}(\mathrm{logits}_{\bar\theta_n})$ is the EMA model output (fully detached), $p^{\theta_n}_n = \mathrm{softmax}(\mathrm{logits}_{\theta_n})$ is the current model output (with grad on $\theta_n$), $b, t$ index batch/token positions, $m_{b,t}$ is attention mask (padding excluded), $v$ indexes vocab (50257 for OPT-125M).

The chain actual contradiction loss form (`contradiction_loss.py` line 213-217 with chain config `cat_arm_b.yaml` overrides $\lambda_i = 1$, $T_2$ form `"relu_dpp"`, $K=1$):

$$\boxed{\;\mathcal{L}_{\rm cont}^{\rm chain,\,actual}(\theta_n) = (\Delta D_n)^2 + (D_n - \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n)^2\;}$$

where:
- $\Delta D_n := D_n - D_{n-1}$ (one-step forward difference, $D_{n-1}$ fully detached)
- $\bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n := \beta_{\rm kl} \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_{n-1} + (1-\beta_{\rm kl}) D_{n-1}$ (EMA recursive update, fully detached on $\theta_n$)
- $\beta_{\rm kl} = 0.9$ (chain config, $\beta_{\rm kl} = e^{-m_{\rm eff,\,chain-config}} = e^{-0.105} \approx 0.9$; note the chain config $m_{\rm eff} = 1.0$ does **not** match this $\beta_{\rm kl}$ because $\beta_{\rm kl}$ is a separate YAML parameter, see §3.2 for explicit chain config decomposition)

**Why two-term, not three-term**: The `T_2_form` chain config is `"relu_dpp"` with the implementation
$$T_2 = \mathrm{ReLU}(D_n - 2 D_{n-1} + D_{n-2})$$
which requires $|D_{\rm history}| \geq 2$. The chain config `kl_history_K = 1` (yaml default missing, schema falls to `getattr(yaml_cat, "kl_history_K", 1) = 1`) limits the history buffer to at most one previous element. Consequently the `if len(D_history) == 1` branch fires and sets `D_doubleprime = torch.zeros_like(D_n)`, yielding $T_2 = \mathrm{ReLU}(0) = 0$ for all generations $n \geq 1$. **The chain therefore runs effectively a two-term loss**, with the velocity term $T_1 = (\Delta D_n)^2$ and the EMA-deviation term $T_3 = (D_n - \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n)^2$ as the only two terms entering loss and contributing to gradient.

**Coefficient values**: $\lambda_1 = \lambda_3 = 1$ (chain config YAML overrides). The Klein-Gordon-style coefficients $\lambda_1 = 1/(2 m_{\rm eff}), \lambda_2 = m_{\rm eff}/2, \lambda_3 = m_{\rm eff}$ are specified in `cat_arm_b_v2_dialectical.yaml` but **that config was never launched in the chain** (`phase1_robust_chain.sh` line 75 hardcodes `--config configs/cat_arm_b.yaml`).

### §3.3 Honest constraint composition (v4 重写,推翻 v3 5/5 LLM domain claim)

**v4 关键 substantive 重构(反题 P0-3 fix)**:

The chain actual two-term form satisfies certain dimensional and structural constraints, but the decomposition is honest rather than the "5/5 axiom-derived from LLM domain" framing in v3 §3.3. The constraints are honestly decomposed as **3 LLM domain axioms + 1 mathematical framework choice + 1 dialectical axiom import**:

#### Constraint 1 (LLM domain) — Causal recurrence

Self-iteration $\theta_n$ depends only on $\theta_{n-k}$ for $k \geq 1$ (causal recursion). LLM auto-regressive generation is inherently causal — gen $n$ samples become gen $n+1$ training data, but gen $n+1$ information cannot backward-influence gen $n$ training.

#### Constraint 2 (LLM domain) — Discrete generation

Generation step $n \in \mathbb{N}$ (discrete). LLM self-iteration is discrete generation cycles (gen 0, gen 1, ...), each cycle is a complete fine-tune pass.

#### Constraint 3 (LLM domain) — Time-reversal-symmetry breaking

LLM generation forward-only, $\theta_n \to \theta_{n+1}$ irreversible. Model collapse is monotonic deterioration (Shumailov 2024 Theorem 1 + Borji 2024 KL stabilization), system cannot spontaneously recover from collapsed state.

#### Constraint 4 (mathematical framework choice) — Quadratic positivity (Lyapunov candidate)

$\mathcal{L}_{\rm cont} \geq 0$ for all $\{D_k\}$ (Lyapunov candidate + Banach contraction metric property). **This is a mathematical framework choice for stability analysis**, not an LLM domain intrinsic constraint. Quartic / higher-order forms are not excluded by LLM domain prior; the exclusion reason is "Lyapunov candidate framework choice + Banach contraction metric prerequisite". Alternative stability frameworks (ISS, contraction analysis, free-energy principle, mirror descent, etc.) would give different family selections.

#### Constraint 5 (dialectical axiom import) — Internal-external dialectical unity

Two-term functional must instantiate **internal cause** (system intrinsic dynamics) + **external cause** (perturbation/regularization) dialectical coupling. **This is a dialectical materialism axiom directly imported**, not derivable from LLM domain. The single-term Lagrangian is excluded by this axiom not by any LLM domain prior.

### §3.6 Mean-field NESS analysis on chain actual two-term form (v4 重写,推翻 v3 Klein-Gordon-coefficient three-term derive)

**v4 关键 substantive 重构(纪律 3 chain align)**:

The chain actual two-term form admits a mean-field NESS Banach contraction analysis with explicit caveats. We re-derive on the chain actual form, replacing v3 §3.6 Klein-Gordon-coefficient three-term derive.

#### §3.6.1 Mean-field gradient (chain actual form)

The gradient of the chain actual two-term loss with respect to $D_n$:

$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_{\rm cont}^{\rm chain,\,actual}}{\partial D_n} = 2(D_n - D_{n-1}) + 2(D_n - \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n) = 2\bigl[2 D_n - D_{n-1} - \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n\bigr]$$

In mean-field approximation $D_{n-1} \approx D^* \approx \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n$ (NESS regime):

$$\frac{\partial \mathcal{L}_{\rm cont}^{\rm chain,\,actual}}{\partial D_n} \approx 4(D_n - D^*)$$

#### §3.6.2 Mean-field SGD update on D space (linearized at NESS attractor)

Assume the LM gradient contribution to $D$ space evolution is $\partial \mathcal{L}_{\rm LM}/\partial D_n \approx -J_S$ (per appendix D.1 form (ii) LM gradient projection on $D$, where $J_S$ is the collapse drift rate). The SGD update in $D$ space:

$$D_{n+1} \approx D_n - \eta \alpha \cdot 4(D_n - D^*) - \eta J_S \cdot (-1) \cdot \mathrm{sign} = D_n - \eta \alpha \cdot 4(D_n - D^*) - \eta J_S$$

Define the Banach map $T(D) = D - 4\eta\alpha(D - D^*) - \eta J_S$. Linearize at $D^*$: set $D_n = D^* + \epsilon_n$:

$$\epsilon_{n+1} = (1 - 4\eta\alpha)\epsilon_n - \eta J_S$$

Inhomogeneous fixed point: $\epsilon^* = -\eta J_S / (4\eta\alpha) = -J_S/(4\alpha)$. Hence:

$$\boxed{\;D^{*,\rm code}(\alpha) = D^* - \frac{J_S}{4\alpha}\;}$$

**v4 关键 substantive 数学结论 vs v3**:
- v3 paper §3.5 stated $D^*(\alpha) = J_S/(\alpha m_{\rm eff})$ derived from Klein-Gordon-coefficient three-term form
- v4 chain actual form gives $D^{*,\rm code}(\alpha) = D^* - J_S/(4\alpha)$ derived from mean-field NESS analysis on two-term form
- **Functional form differs**: v3 $\propto 1/(\alpha m_{\rm eff})$ requires $m_{\rm eff}$ dependence; v4 $\propto 1/\alpha$ does **not** require $m_{\rm eff}$ dependence (because $m_{\rm eff}$ does not enter chain actual two-term loss)
- The $D^*$ value in v4 is read off from chain plateau test-perplexity values via empirical fit ($D^* \approx \log(55) \approx 4.0$ nat/token at α=10), not from a $J_S/(\alpha m_{\rm eff})$ closed-form

#### §3.6.3 Banach contraction Lipschitz (chain actual form)

The Banach map $T(D) = D - 4\eta\alpha(D - D^*) - \eta J_S$ has Lipschitz constant:

$$|T(D_1) - T(D_2)| = |D_1 - D_2| \cdot |1 - 4\eta\alpha|$$

Contraction $\Leftrightarrow |1 - 4\eta\alpha| < 1 \Leftrightarrow 0 < \eta\alpha < 1/2$.

Chain config values: $\eta = 2 \times 10^{-5}$ (AdamW learning rate), $\alpha = 10$. Then $\eta\alpha = 2 \times 10^{-4} \ll 1/2$, so:

$$\rho^{\rm code,\,per-train-step} = 1 - 4\eta\alpha = 1 - 8 \times 10^{-4} = 0.99920$$

Per-train-step half-convergence: $n_{1/2}^{\rm train-step} = \log 0.5 / \log 0.99920 = 866$ train steps $\approx 0.6$ epoch (chain 1460 steps/epoch).

**Per-generation compound**: Single generation = 1460 train steps. Contradiction loss compute every 10 train steps (`kl_update_every = 10`), giving 146 contradiction-loss-active updates per generation. Per-generation Banach contraction ratio:

$$\rho^{\rm code,\,per-gen} = (1 - 4\eta\alpha)^{146} = 0.99920^{146} = 0.890$$

Per-generation half-convergence: $n_{1/2}^{\rm gen} = \log 0.5 / \log 0.890 = 5.94$ generations.

After 9 generations: $(\rho^{\rm code,\,per-gen})^9 = 0.890^9 = 0.347$ (34.7% residual).
