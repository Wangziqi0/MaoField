### §2.1 工具 1 — NESS 变分原理 / Schwinger-Keldysh / MSR 作用量

#### §2.1.1 严格 statement + 假设 + 文献 reference

**核心 statement** (MSR / Janssen / De Dominicis path integral formalism):

给随机微分方程 (Stratonovich form):
$$
\dot\phi(x, t) = F[\phi](x, t) + \eta(x, t), \quad \langle\eta(x, t)\eta(x', t')\rangle = 2D\delta(x-x')\delta(t-t')
$$
其 path integral generating functional $Z[J, \tilde J]$ 可写为 MSR action functional 形式:
$$
Z[J, \tilde J] = \int \mathcal{D}\phi \mathcal{D}\tilde\phi \exp\left\{-S[\phi, \tilde\phi] + \int dx dt (J\phi + \tilde J \tilde\phi)\right\}
$$
其中 MSR action functional 严格 form:
$$
S[\phi, \tilde\phi] = \int dx dt \left\{\tilde\phi(\partial_t\phi - F[\phi]) - D\tilde\phi^2\right\}
$$
$\tilde\phi$ 是 **response field** (auxiliary conjugate field), MSR 1973 引入. Janssen 1976 + De Dominicis 1976 + Peliti 推 path integral 起点严格化.

**关键假设 (严格 list)**:
1. **Markovian dynamics**: SDE $\dot\phi = F[\phi] + \eta$ 是 Markov (无 memory kernel, 即 $\eta$ delta-correlated in time)
2. **Gaussian white noise**: $\eta$ 是 zero-mean Gaussian 且 covariance 是 delta function (otherwise path integral measure 复杂)
3. **Detailed balance** (optional): 若 NESS 是平衡态, detailed balance 给 fluctuation-dissipation theorem; **非平衡态 (NESS)** detailed balance 破缺
4. **Gauge invariance**: response field $\tilde\phi$ 的 normalization gauge ambiguity 必须 fix (Janssen 1976 fix gauge by ${\rm det}(\partial_t - \delta F/\delta\phi) = 1$ Itô convention)

**主要文献 reference** (全可验证):

### §2.2 工具 2 — Foster-Lyapunov drift criterion

#### §2.2.1 严格 statement + 假设 + 文献 reference

**核心 statement** (Foster-Lyapunov 标准 form, Foster 1953 + Meyn-Tweedie 1993):

设 $\{\theta_n\}$ 是 ψ-不可约 Markov 链 on state space $\Theta$. 若存在 measurable function $V: \Theta \to [0, +\infty)$ + small set $C$ + 常数 $\beta > 0, b < \infty$ s.t. **drift inequality**:
$$
\mathbb{E}[V(\theta_{n+1}) | \theta_n] - V(\theta_n) \le -\beta V(\theta_n) + b \mathbf{1}_C(\theta_n) \quad \forall \theta_n \in \Theta
$$
则 Markov 链 **geometric ergodic**: 存在 unique stationary distribution $\pi$ + constants $\rho \in (0, 1), M < \infty$ s.t.
$$
\sup_{|f| \le V + 1} |\mathbb{E}_{\theta_0}[f(\theta_n)] - \pi(f)| \le M \cdot V(\theta_0) \cdot \rho^n
$$
(Meyn-Tweedie 1993 Theorem 15.0.1).

**Polyak-Łojasiewicz (PL) 不等式** (与 Foster-Lyapunov drift 关联的 optimization side):
$$
\|\nabla f(\theta)\|^2 \ge 2\mu (f(\theta) - f^*) \quad \forall \theta \in \Theta
$$
其中 $f^* = \min_\theta f(\theta)$. PL 不等式 + $L$-smooth + SGD update → geometric convergence (Karimi-Nutini-Schmidt 2016 Theorem 1).

**关键假设 (严格 list)**:
1. **ψ-不可约 Markov 链**: 存在 σ-finite measure $\psi$ on $\Theta$ s.t. $\forall A: \psi(A) > 0, \exists n: T_H^n(\theta, A) > 0$ for all $\theta \in \Theta$
2. **small set $C \subseteq \Theta$ 存在**: small-set Doeblin condition: $\exists \epsilon > 0, n_0 \in \mathbb{N}, \nu$ 概率 measure s.t. $T_H^{n_0}(\theta, A) \ge \epsilon \nu(A)$ for $\theta \in C, A \subseteq \Theta$
3. **drift inequality**: $V$ Lyapunov function 满足上式
4. **aperiodicity** (optional, for total variation convergence rate): chain 不周期
5. **PL 不等式 if loss landscape based Lyapunov**: $V = f$ loss function 需满足 PL with $\mu$-constant

**主要文献 reference** (全可验证):
- **Foster 1953** F.G. Foster, *On the stochastic matrices associated with certain queueing processes*, Ann. Math. Stat. 24, 355 (1953), DOI: 10.1214/aoms/1177728976. 原文 introduce drift criterion.
- **Meyn-Tweedie 1993** S.P. Meyn, R.L. Tweedie, *Markov Chains and Stochastic Stability*, Springer-Verlag (1993), 2nd edition Cambridge University Press 2009, ISBN 978-0-521-73182-9 (paperback). 教材 standard reference, Chapter 15 geometric ergodicity. Cambridge 2009 reprint 增 P.W. Glynn 前言.

Mattingly, *Yet Another Look at Harris' Ergodic Theorem for Markov Chains*, [arXiv:0810.2777](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.2777), Seminar on Stochastic Analysis, Random Fields and Applications VI, Progress in Probability 63 (Birkhäuser 2011), DOI: 10.1007/978-3-0348-0021-1_7.

### §2.3 工具 3 — Banach 不动点定理

#### §2.3.1 严格 statement + 假设 + 文献 reference

**核心 statement** (Banach 1922 原文):

设 $(X, d)$ 是 complete metric space, $T: X \to X$ 是 contraction mapping, 即 $\exists \rho \in [0, 1)$ s.t.
$$
d(T(x), T(y)) \le \rho \cdot d(x, y) \quad \forall x, y \in X
$$
则:
1. **唯一不动点存在**: $\exists ! x^* \in X$ s.t. $T(x^*) = x^*$
2. **geometric 收敛**: 任意 $x_0 \in X$, 序列 $x_n := T(x_{n-1})$ 收敛到 $x^*$ with rate
$$
d(x_n, x^*) \le \rho^n \cdot d(x_0, x^*)
$$

**关键假设 (严格 list)**:
1. **Complete metric space**: $(X, d)$ 是 metric space (满足 metric 4 axioms) + 是 complete (Cauchy 序列必收敛 in $X$)
2. **Self-map**: $T: X \to X$ (image stays in $X$)
3. **Lipschitz continuous with constant $\rho < 1$**: $d(T(x), T(y)) \le \rho d(x, y)$ 严格 strict, $\rho < 1$ (not $\le 1$, otherwise non-contractive)

**主要文献 reference** (全可验证):
- **Banach 1922** S. Banach, *Sur les opérations dans les ensembles abstraits et leur application aux équations intégrales*, Fund. Math. 3, 133 (1922). 原文 prove contraction mapping theorem.
- **Kreyszig 1978** E. Kreyszig, *Introductory Functional Analysis with Applications*, John Wiley & Sons (1978), ISBN 978-0-471-50459-7. 教材 §5.1 标准 statement + proof.
- **Conway 1990** J.B. Conway, *A Course in Functional Analysis*, 2nd edition, Springer-Verlag (1990), ISBN 978-0-387-97245-9. 教材 Chapter 6 standard reference.
- **Granas-Dugundji 2003** A. Granas, J. Dugundji, *Fixed Point Theory*, Springer-Verlag (2003), ISBN 978-0-387-00173-9. 系统 fixed point theory monograph.

### §2.4 工具 4 — Volterra 积分方程

#### §2.4.1 严格 statement + 假设 + 文献 reference

**核心 statement** (Volterra 第二类积分方程, Volterra 1930 原文 + Tricomi 1957 教材):

Volterra 第二类线性积分方程:
$$
\phi(t) = f(t) + \int_a^t K(t, s) \phi(s) ds
$$
其中 $K(t, s)$ 是 Volterra kernel (causal: $K(t, s) = 0$ for $s > t$), $f(t)$ 是 inhomogeneous term.

**关键性质 (严格 list)**:
1. **存在唯一性** (Tricomi 1957 教材 Chapter II): 若 $K$ 在 $[a, b] \times [a, b]$ 连续 + $f$ 连续, $\phi$ 唯一连续解存在 by Picard iteration
2. **Neumann 级数 expansion**: $\phi = f + Kf + K^2 f + \ldots$ 收敛在 sup norm
3. **causal Green function form** (Klein-Gordon Green function standard): 对方程 $(\partial_t + m) \phi = f$, retarded Green function $G_{\rm ret}(t) = e^{-mt} \mathbf{1}[t \ge 0]$, $\phi(t) = \int_0^\infty G_{\rm ret}(\tau) f(t - \tau) d\tau$

**关键假设 (严格 list)**:
1. **Causal kernel**: $K(t, s) = 0$ for $s > t$ (Volterra "下三角" property)
2. **连续 + bounded**: $K$ 在 compact interval 连续 + bounded
3. **Lipschitz on $\phi$** (for nonlinear Volterra): $|K(t, s, \phi_1) - K(t, s, \phi_2)| \le L|\phi_1 - \phi_2|$ guarantee Picard 收敛

**主要文献 reference** (全可验证):

### §2.6 工具 6 — Markov 链 ψ-不可约 + Doeblin 条件

#### §2.6.1 严格 statement + 假设 + 文献 reference

**核心 statement** (Meyn-Tweedie 1993 教材 + Roberts-Rosenthal 2004 review):

**ψ-不可约**: Markov chain $\{X_n\}$ on state space $(X, \mathcal{F})$ 是 ψ-不可约 if 存在 σ-finite measure $\psi$ on $(X, \mathcal{F})$ s.t.
$$
\forall A \in \mathcal{F}: \psi(A) > 0 \Rightarrow \forall x \in X, \exists n \in \mathbb{N}: P^n(x, A) > 0
$$
即对 ψ-正测集 $A$, chain 从任何起点 $x$ 都能 in finitely many steps 进入 $A$ with positive probability.

**Doeblin condition (minorization)**: 存在 $\epsilon > 0, n_0 \in \mathbb{N}, \nu$ 概率 measure on $(X, \mathcal{F})$ s.t.
$$
P^{n_0}(x, A) \ge \epsilon \nu(A) \quad \forall x \in X, A \in \mathcal{F}
$$

**Small set**: $C \in \mathcal{F}$ 是 small if 存在 $\epsilon > 0, n_0 \in \mathbb{N}, \nu$ 概率 measure s.t.
$$
P^{n_0}(x, A) \ge \epsilon \nu(A) \quad \forall x \in C, A \in \mathcal{F}
$$

### §2.9 工具 9 — KL divergence 在 ML / LLM 域

#### §2.9.1 严格 statement + 假设 + 文献 reference

**核心 statement** (Kullback-Leibler divergence 标准 form):

设 $P, Q$ 是 probability distributions on $(X, \mathcal{F})$ s.t. $P \ll Q$ (absolutely continuous), KL divergence:
$$
{\rm KL}(P \| Q) = \int_X \log\left(\frac{dP}{dQ}\right) dP
$$
若 $P, Q$ 是 discrete: ${\rm KL}(P\|Q) = \sum_x P(x) \log(P(x)/Q(x))$.

**性质**:
1. **Non-negativity**: ${\rm KL}(P\|Q) \ge 0$, $= 0$ iff $P = Q$ a.e.
2. **Non-symmetric**: ${\rm KL}(P\|Q) \neq {\rm KL}(Q\|P)$ generically
3. **Convexity**: ${\rm KL}$ jointly convex in $(P, Q)$
4. **Mode-covering**: ${\rm KL}(P \| Q_\theta)$ 最小化 (即 $P$ 是 reference, $Q_\theta$ trained) 倾向 mode-covering (Q 必 cover P 所有 support)
5. **Mode-seeking**: ${\rm KL}(Q_\theta \| P)$ 最小化倾向 mode-seeking (Q 集中在 P 高概率 region)

**Wasserstein 比较**: KL 散度对小 perturbation sensitive, Wasserstein distance 对 metric 结构 sensitive (Earth Mover's Distance), 在 LLM 域 generative model evaluation 两者 trade-off.

**主要文献 reference** (全可验证):
- **Kullback-Leibler 1951** S. Kullback, R.A. Leibler, *On Information and Sufficiency*, Ann. Math. Stat. 22, 79 (1951), DOI: 10.1214/aoms/1177729694. 原文 KL divergence 定义.
- **Cover-Thomas 2006** T.M. Cover, J.A. Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, 2nd edition Wiley (2006), ISBN 978-0-471-24195-9. 教材 §2 KL divergence + information theory standard.
- **Tishby-Pereira-Bialek 1999** N. Tishby, F. Pereira, W. Bialek, *The Information Bottleneck Method*, [arXiv:physics/0004057](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0004057), Proc. 37th Annual Allerton Conference on Communication, Control and Computing (1999), pages 368-377. Information bottleneck principle.
- **Shumailov 2024** I. Shumailov, Z. Shumaylov, Y. Zhao, et al., *AI Models Collapse When Trained on Recursively Generated Data*, Nature 631, 755 (2024), DOI: 10.1038/s41586-024-07566-y. KL divergence 在 LLM 模型崩溃 trajectory 量化.
- **Borji 2024** A. Borji, *A Note on Shumailov et al. (2024): AI Models Collapse When Trained on Recursively Generated Data*, [arXiv:2410.12954](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12954). KL relaxation timescale + KDE analysis.
