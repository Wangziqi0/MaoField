## Title

**Dialectical Contradiction Loss for Self-Iteration Collapse Mitigation in Large Language Models: A Constraint-Driven Framework with Multi-Seed Empirical Verification**

## Abstract

**Background.** Self-iteration model collapse in large language models (LLMs), as documented by Shumailov et al. (2024, *Nature*), describes the catastrophic perplexity degradation when models are recursively trained on their own generations. Borji (2024) surfaces an unexplained empirical anomaly: Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence stabilizes within a finite range across iterations while Wasserstein distance continuously grows — a phenomenon for which the existing model-collapse framing offers no quantitative mechanism. Dohmatob (2025) provides a Markov-chain-level $\zeta$-extra-term analysis specific to linear regression, but does not explain the KL stabilization observed in language modeling.

**Approach.** We propose a constraint-driven contradiction Lagrangian $\mathcal{L}_{\rm contradiction}^{\rm Hartree}$ as a regularization term for the self-iteration loss. Five axioms from the LLM domain (causal recurrence, discrete generation, time-reversal symmetry breaking, quadratic positivity, internal-external dialectical unity) narrow the ansatz space to 2-3 families satisfying all constraints. The actual implemented (and theoretically analyzed) form consists of three terms: a velocity term $\lambda_1 (\Delta D_n)^2$, an exponential-moving-average (EMA) deviation term $\lambda_2 (D_n - \bar{D}^{\rm EMA}_n)^2$, and a quadratic-mass term $\lambda_3 D_n^2 / 2$, where $D_n$ is the relative KL divergence with respect to the gen-0 baseline. Three main theorems (Markov topology change, NESS Hartree fixed-point attractor, geometric convergence rate) are stated conditional on assumptions A1-A12 (eight standard SGD-analysis assumptions, three framework-specific assumptions, and one leading-order linearization assumption).

**Findings.** Multi-seed Phase 1 chain (N=4, seeds 1-4; seed=0 systematically excluded due to documented hardware-environment failures and disclosed in §4.1) on OPT-125M with WikiText-2 yields:
- $m_{\rm eff} = 0.300 \pm 0.066$ (mean ± 95% CI half-width, Student-t df=3), an empirically fitted relaxation rate parameter (not a derived physical constant);
- $J_S^{(2)} = 0.535 \pm 0.005$ nat/token/generation (slope gen 0→2 to peak, with sensitivity to estimator choice spanning $J_S \in [0.330, 0.770]$ across three methods);
- Partial D4 shape robustness 5/5 PASS (STRONG ROBUST) across both $\alpha = 0$ baseline and $\alpha = 10$ framework chains (U-shape pattern + low gen-0 baseline CV + spike + plateau + sliding-window consistency);
- F3 NOT substantiated (paired difference of $\alpha = 10$ vs $\alpha = 0$ plateau perplexity: mean $-0.57\%$, $p_{\rm two} = 0.82$, N=4 paired test-perplexity);
- Framework prediction of stationary perplexity ${\rm PPL}_\infty = 43.4 \pm 1.7$ from $D^*(\alpha=10) = J_S / (\alpha m_{\rm eff}) = 0.178$ nat/token vs measured $56.1 \pm 2.4$, a $+29\%$ discrepancy ($z \approx 4.3\sigma$) honestly disclosed as a candidate falsification signal.

**Future work.** F-1 Phase 2 universal uniqueness theorem (excluding 8+ remaining ansatz families) is deferred to 2-4 weeks substantive work. Multi-architecture verification (Llama, Pythia) and multi-seed N $\geq$ 8 paired-test for resolution of the F3 verdict are deferred 1-2 months and 2-3 weeks respectively. Closure of the +29% absolute level discrepancy is deferred to multi-architecture $H(q_*)$ empirical fit.

**Honesty disclosure.** The framework's mathematical scaffolding (NESS Hartree variational closure, Banach contraction, Foster-Lyapunov drift, EMA-deviation memory) was developed via cross-domain import from condensed-matter and non-equilibrium field theory. The mapping to Mao's *On Contradiction* (1937) and Lenin's *Materialism and Empirio-Criticism* (1908) in §7 is a retrospective dialectical framing of the derived mathematical structure, not an axiom-first derivation. We argue (§1.2 + §3) that the dialectical materialist axiom is *logically sufficient* to derive the form within the restricted ansatz space defined by the five constraints; the historical development order was the inverse.

### §3.8 RLHF axis $\mathcal{L}_{\rm contradiction}^{\rm Hartree}$ 扩展 (保留 v2 substantive)

**v3 binding**: §3.8 保留 v2 substantive content (基于 MATH_LAYER_RLHF_DPPL §1 4 步 axiom→form derive 链 integrate). RLHF axis $\mathcal{L}_{\rm contradiction}^{\rm Hartree}$ form 在 SFT axis 同 form (Family 1 EMA-deviation instantiation 或 Family 2 Volterra form) under 假设 A1-A7-RLHF.

The framework's contradiction Lagrangian extends from the SFT axis (maximum-likelihood self-iteration) to the RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback) axis. The key difference is the introduction of a reward signal $r(\theta)$ as the external cause ($\mathcal{F}_{\rm ex}^{\rm RLHF}$), while the internal cause ($\mathcal{F}_{\rm in}^{\rm RLHF}$) remains the model's intrinsic dialectical reflective capacity. By Axiom 2 (internal-external dialectics inseparable), these three components (internal, external, coupling) are unified into:

$$
\boxed{\;\mathcal{L}_{\rm contradiction}^{\rm RLHF, Hartree}(\theta; n) = \lambda_1^{\rm RLHF} (\Delta D^{\rm RLHF}_n)^2 + \lambda_2^{\rm RLHF} (D^{\rm RLHF}_n - \bar{D}^{\rm RLHF, EMA}_n)^2 + \lambda_3^{\rm RLHF} \cdot \frac{(D^{\rm RLHF}_n)^2}{2}\;}
$$

(v3 align code form Family 1a EMA-deviation instantiation for RLHF axis too)

where $D^{\rm RLHF}_n$ is defined as a weighted scalar projection of the warmth-honesty dual:
$$
D^{\rm RLHF}_n := w_{\rm warm} D_{\rm warmth}^n + w_{\rm honest} D_{\rm honesty}^n
$$
$D_{\rm warmth}^n := D_{\rm KL}(p_{\theta_n} \| p_{\rm warm\text{-}target})$, $D_{\rm honesty}^n := D_{\rm KL}(p_{\theta_n} \| p_{\rm honest\text{-}target})$.

The Hartree mean-field NESS variational closure (Tauber 2014, Kamenev 2011) gives the effective mass on the RLHF axis:
$$
m_{\rm eff}^{\rm RLHF, eff}(\beta) = m_{\rm eff} \cdot (1 + c\beta)
$$
where $\beta$ is the standard RLHF KL penalty coefficient and $c$ is a dimensionless perturbative coefficient that captures the additional restoring force from the reward-policy KL contraction. The coefficients $\lambda_i^{\rm RLHF}$ cascade via $\lambda_1^{\rm RLHF} = 1/(2 m_{\rm eff}^{\rm RLHF, eff})$, $\lambda_2^{\rm RLHF} = m_{\rm eff}^{\rm RLHF, eff}/2$, $\lambda_3^{\rm RLHF} = m_{\rm eff}^{\rm RLHF, eff}$.

**Connection to Ibrahim et al. 2026 Nature warmth-honesty trade-off.** The framework's internal-external unification (Axiom 2) and Hartree dressed mass together yield the Ibrahim warmth-honesty trade-off as a derived corollary. $$
\frac{d \langle (\delta D_{\rm honesty})^2 \rangle}{d w_{\rm warm}} = -\frac{2}{m_{\rm honesty}^{2, \rm eff}} \langle r_{\rm warm}, r_{\rm honest} \rangle < 0
$$
when the warmth and honesty reward functions are negatively correlated ($\langle r_{\rm warm}, r_{\rm honest} \rangle < 0$), consistent with Ibrahim et al. 2026 (DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10410-0, arXiv 2507.21919) mixed-effects model finding. This derivation provides a candidate *a priori* mathematical mechanism for the Ibrahim trade-off; the empirical observation by Ibrahim is recovered as a specific instantiation of Axiom 2 on the RLHF axis with negatively-correlated warmth-honesty reward functions.

## §6 D-PPL conversion bridge (集成 2 新段, 数学层第二层 input, 继承 v2)

### §6.1 严格 statement (P0-2 cascade)

In framework, $D_n$ is defined as the **relative** KL divergence with respect to the baseline gen-0 model on the test set, **not** an absolute KL:
$$
\boxed{\;D_n^{\rm relative} := \log\left(\frac{{\rm PPL}_n}{{\rm PPL}_0}\right) = D_{\rm KL}(q_* \| p_{\theta_n}) - D_{\rm KL}(q_* \| p_{\theta_0})\;}
$$
where $q_*$ is the test set ground-truth distribution and ${\rm PPL}_n$ is the standard test set perplexity at generation $n$ (definition by Shumailov 2024).

The differential form is rigorously derived from the cross-entropy decomposition $H(q_*, p) = H(q_*) + D_{\rm KL}(q_* \| p)$ (Cover-Thomas 2006) under the i.i.d. assumption on test set tokens (Strong law of large numbers). The unit of $D_n$ is **[nat/token]** (token-averaged), consistent with the PPL definition. The framework explicitly assumes $H(q_*) \approx {\rm const}$ (test set distribution fixed across generations), which is the standard empirical setup of model collapse studies.

### §6.2 Reference clarity binding disclose

We emphasize that $D_n$ as defined here is **relative** to the gen-0 baseline, **not** the absolute KL divergence to $q_*$. The absolute KL
$$
D_{\rm KL}(q_* \| p_{\theta_n}) = \log {\rm PPL}_n - H(q_*)
$$
is not directly observable because $H(q_*)$ requires knowledge of the exact ground-truth distribution; in practice, ${\rm PPL}_0 \approx 36$ (OPT-125M on WikiText-2 test set) implies $D_{\rm KL}(q_* \| p_{\theta_0}) \ne 0$, confirming the baseline model has not converged to $q_*$. We defer absolute $D_n$ characterization (via multi-architecture $H(q_*)$ empirical fit) to future work F8.2 (1-2 weeks substantive).

All framework numerical predictions (§3.2 m_eff multi-seed + §3.6.5 J_S multi-seed + §3.6 D*(α) cascade) are stated with respect to the relative form. The framework's quantitative prediction
$$
D^*(\alpha=10) = \frac{J_S^{(2)}}{10 m_{\rm eff}} = \frac{0.5351}{3.000} = 0.178 \text{ nat/token}, \quad 95\% \text{ CI } [0.145, 0.231]
$$
predicts a long-run NESS attractor PPL stationary value ${\rm PPL}_\infty = {\rm PPL}_0 \cdot \exp(D^*(10)) = 43.4 \pm 1.7$ relative to gen-0 baseline.

#### D.1 $J_S$ 严格定义

$J_S$ 定义 (alternative 等价 forms):
- (i) collapse drift rate: $J_S := dD/dn |_{n \to 0^+}$ in baseline α=0 regime (实测)
- (ii) LM gradient projection: $J_S := -\nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\rm LM} \cdot \nabla_\theta D / \|\nabla_\theta D\|^2$ (derive)

两 form 量纲一致 [nat/token/generation], (i) 是 empirical fit, (ii) 是 derive (从 SGD update equation).

#### D.2 三方法 estimator

**Method 1 (slope gen 0 → gen 1)**:
$$
J_S^{(1, {\rm seed})} := D_n^{\rm seed}[1] - D_n^{\rm seed}[0]
$$

**Method 2 (slope gen 0 → gen 2 to peak)**:
$$
J_S^{(2, {\rm seed})} := \frac{D_n^{\rm seed}[2] - D_n^{\rm seed}[0]}{2}
$$

**Method 3 (mean rate gen 0 → gen 3)**:
$$
J_S^{(3, {\rm seed})} := \frac{1}{3}\sum_{k=0}^{2}\left(D_n^{\rm seed}[k+1] - D_n^{\rm seed}[k]\right) = \frac{D_n^{\rm seed}[3] - D_n^{\rm seed}[0]}{3}
$$
