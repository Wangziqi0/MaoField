## Strongest defensible conclusion

A fully preregistered, all-six-or-none causal test did not establish causal inheritance of falsification histories. More importantly, it exposed a three-way separation between execution validity, treatment-effect identification, and construct validity: sealed execution and exact statistics can coexist with non-evaluable behaviour; exact zero contrasts can coexist with failed positive controls; and a large replicated cross-channel gap can fail the independent criterion gates required for a dissociation. The publishable contribution is therefore a measurement result, not a positive HFI or structural-deficit result.

## Claim ceiling

Allowed: registered treatment invariance for three local conditions; objective non-evaluability for Mistral and both DeepSeek modes; failure of ordinary and structure positive controls; failure to establish P1/P2/P3/P4/P5; anti-clustered replication of the same boundary; and the methodological separation of execution, criterion validity and causal interpretation.

Forbidden: no HFI in LLMs; flat state is sufficient; Transformer structural defect; universal action–structure dissociation; DeepSeek reasoning-mode reversal; model ranking; AGI or philosophical theorem.
