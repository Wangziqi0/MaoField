# Supplementary Note 2 | Construct and causal design

A practice-grounded falsification history was represented as `R/P/E/S/K`: a rejected route, decisive practice, external evidence, scope and reopening condition. Five randomized history arms were delivered to fresh clean successors: `DN` (correctly bound history), `UF` (typed-edge-rewired relation removal), `X` (coherent wrong grounding), `CO` (current conclusion without the historical relation), and `N0` (matched no-history condition). Four future cells imposed non-compensatory duties: Stable, Continuity, reopening inactive (`R0`) and reopening active (`R1`).

The primary response was `U`, the pass fraction on executable sealed hidden tests after a complete tool trajectory. `A_FT`, informative-probe selection `P`, ordered observation use `O`, resources and typed trajectory failures were objective secondary endpoints. Mechanism claims used an independent fixed-probe/action sibling `A_SIB` and an independent relation readout `R`. Structure and interface channels never gated collection or reporting of Direct objective ITT.

A full HFI result required the intersection of Stable equivalence, Continuity specificity, selective reopening, rejection of wrong grounding, and separation from conclusion-only and no-history controls. No pooled or compensatory score was permitted.

## Formal theory and proposition inheritance

The complete MaoField chain was:

```text
P0 practice-grounded scientific history
→ R_fals=(R,P,E,S,K)
→ prespecified current-content map C_flat
→ future intervention response law Ψ_h(i)
→ system-side closure defect D_I(C)
→ clean-successor causal experiment
→ Direct P1/P5
→ independent A_SIB and M_R channels
→ P2/P3/P4
→ evaluator kernel K_i
→ evaluator-visible defect D_I^K(C)
→ claim-specific separation and claim ceiling.
```

P0 defined scientific history as a relation made objective by practice, not as a token sequence, a fact list or a present conclusion. The prespecified map `C_flat` retained evaluator-visible typed atoms, current conclusion and history-insensitive disposition. For a history `h` and future intervention `i`, `Ψ_h(i)` denoted the true future response law. The system-side intervention-closure defect was

**D_I(C) = sup_{C(h)=C(h′), i∈I} d_Y(Ψ_h(i), Ψ_h′(i)).**

P1 tested a finite clean-successor behavioural signature; P5 interpreted a valid future-response divergence as a bounded witness of nonclosure, not as a general theorem. P2 and P3 required independent, criterion-valid `A_SIB` and structural `M_R` channels; P4 additionally required cross-family and external-provider replication. P6 governed anti-clustered intervention coverage rather than effect direction.

An evaluator kernel `K_i` mapped true trajectories or structures to observed readouts. Its visible defect was

**D_I^K(C) = sup_{C(h)=C(h′), i∈I} d_Z(K_i#Ψ_h(i), K_i#Ψ_h′(i)).**

For contracted distances, `D_I^K(C) <= D_I(C)`, with no converse unless the evaluator is separating on the response family relevant to the claim. This identifies the role of measurement theory: it is the bridge from observations to system-side propositions, not the complete ontology of scientific history.

The evidential statuses were distinct. P0–P6 and the clean-successor design were prospective before execution. The Phase 23 and GLM tests were confirmatory under their frozen contracts. The structure-estimand counterexample was outcome-aware and post-hoc; it explained non-identification but did not create a positive result. Sharper deficiency bounds, minimal separating evaluator families and long-horizon closure theory remain Article-2 seeds, not established claims.

# Supplementary Note 5 | Statistical analysis and result-tree discipline

The unit of inference was the paired world superblock. Positive constituents used studentized paired randomization/sign-flip tests with 100,000 frozen permutations. Equivalence constituents used two shifted one-sided randomization-compatible tests. Precision intervals used 50,000 paired-cluster bootstrap resamples, with simultaneous critical values where registered. The complete HFI signature was an intersection-union test whose decision was determined by the least favourable constituent; no pooled score could rescue a failed duty. Holm correction was applied within frozen endpoint and claim families, not across models by pooling.

Typed failures were preserved. Transport missingness, malformed output, schema violations, invalid tool order and other registered failures entered the frozen missingness or ITT mapping. Complete-case rescue, result-dependent model exclusion and threshold changes were forbidden.

Main-96 was frozen and fsync/readback-complete before the first scientific read of sealed-32. The sealed layer could replicate or fail to replicate a registered pattern but could not redefine it. Replication stabilized the observed statistic; it did not supply missing operating-range or criterion validity and therefore could not create scientific meaning absent from the measurement bridge.

# Supplementary Note 7 | Post-hoc identification audit

**Status: outcome-aware, explicitly post-hoc.** This audit did not modify the frozen analysis or result labels.

The audit distinguished system-side historical sufficiency from evaluator-side identifiability. Let `C(H)` be a system-side coarse state and `Psi_h(i)` the true future response law. Define

**D_I(C) = sup_{C(h)=C(h′), i∈I} d_Y(Ψ_h(i), Ψ_h′(i)).**

For an evaluator kernel `K_i`, the evaluator-visible defect is

**D_I^K(C) = sup_{C(h)=C(h′), i∈I} d_Z(K_i#Ψ_h(i), K_i#Ψ_h′(i)).**

For distances contracted by the evaluator, `D_I^K(C) <= D_I(C)`. Observed equality therefore has no converse unless the evaluator is separating on the response family relevant to the claim.

The audit also found a non-monotonicity in the Phase 23 `R` accuracy contrast. Perfect arm-specific structure recovery can give `R_DN=R_UF=1` and hence a zero contrast. An arm-blind constant-true response can give `R_DN=1`, `R_UF=0` and hence a maximal positive contrast. The frozen criterion gate correctly blocked overclaiming; the audit shows why the observed cross-channel gap cannot be interpreted as an identified latent dissociation.

This is an identification result, not a reanalysis for significance. The central principle is: **equality under a prespecified current-content map, or equality of evaluator readouts, does not establish intervention equality unless the evaluator has been shown to separate the relevant future response laws.**

# Supplementary Note 8 | Closest-prior boundary and novelty ceiling

The broad propositions that construct validity can fail, reliability can coexist with invalidity, self-report can dissociate from behaviour, and benchmark choice can shape visible representations are occupied by prior work. The retained empirical residual is narrower: within one frozen causal protocol, a null-like exact extreme and a dissociation-like near-saturated extreme recurred in held-out worlds, while the same preregistered result tree rejected both interpretations. The prospective GLM branch then showed that ordinary behavioural calibration can be valid while the history-sensitive full signature and structure criterion remain unresolved. This excludes a single universal ordinary-task incapacity explanation but does not retroactively validate Phase 23 channels.

Allowed priority language is therefore bounded: no directly equivalent primary protocol was identified that combines design-fixed current-content equality, randomized practice-grounded history binding and grounding, Stable-Continuity-Reopening duties, clean successors, executable terminal criteria, sealed anti-clustered replication, and independent calibration for null and dissociation claims. The manuscript does not claim the first construct-validity critique, the first self-report-behaviour gap, universal benchmark collapse, or a universal LLM mechanism.

# Supplementary Note 9 | Claims not made

The Article does not establish that AI scientists generally inherit or fail to inherit falsification; that the current coarse state is interventionally sufficient; that Phase 23 or GLM established finite nonclosure; that Qwen3-14B or GLM demonstrated a causal action-structure dissociation; that `NOT_IDENTIFIED` is a strict null; that ordinary behavioural calibration validates structure; that all LLM benchmarks are invalid; that all LLMs share a measurement-collapse mechanism; that Transformer architectures impose the observed boundary; or that any AGI or philosophical theorem follows.
