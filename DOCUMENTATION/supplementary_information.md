## Supplementary Note 1. Claim architecture

The manuscript combines a theoretical target, an analytical witness and a local recorded application. They do not supply three replications of one mechanism. The title concerns equivalence under a declared present observation and non-equivalence under a declared future regime. It does not assert that identical complete causal states yield different outcomes. “Scientific futures” includes testing, correction, question formation, method development and adaptation under specified duties, tools, permissions and resources; these components need different observations. The proof-completion pair observes local application and supplied downstream use. Separate archived coefficient studies observe actual changed-target revision and reuse; their aggregate policy effects remain unestablished.

The theory takes problem–method–evidence–division-of-work relations as an explanatory object. Some relations are instantiated by a proof interface or a parameter map; others concern responsibility and access. They can be included in a classical state model. The proposal is not an irreducible force outside such a model. Its target is the formation and reconstruction of conditions by activity, rather than assuming those conditions to be permanently supplied from outside.

A minimal evidence discipline for a relation has four parts: identify its carrier; specify the operation for which it matters; observe its actual use or specify a proof of dependence; and distinguish how it was formed from how it was handed over. Adding an omitted variable to a diagram does not establish these parts. Conversely, an intervention need not isolate every internal psychological mechanism to establish a bounded total effect. The proof-completion pair and post-selected successor traces establish particular objects, changes and uses. They do not establish a stable average benefit: the complete coefficient-study results include identical versions, empty terminal outputs and execution-dependent contrasts.

## Supplementary Note 2. Exact finite separation

### 2.1 Original comparison and analytical scope

This argument is inherited from the preserved R6 mathematical note of 18 September 2026, with its assumptions retained. The original note remains in the authors’ internal historical provenance; the complete argument and its assumptions are reproduced here and mapped to the executable checks in `REPRODUCIBILITY/math/README.md`. In a local covariance reduction, the actual amplitude relative to a fixed primary coefficient is $z$, the target squared amplitude is $p$, and the current reported residual is $r=p-z^2$. The update is a real increment $g$ and the complete residual is $E=(z+g)^2-p$. The stored or inherited state is not reset between operations. With $u=z-1$,

$$E=2g+g^2-r+2ug.$$

This is an exact identity, not a behavioural model of the language model. It separates the residual of a correction at the fixed base from its cross term with the actual state already produced. The local reduction inherits a role from the source covariance construction, but it removes other components of the full PDE problem. A small value here does not prove a small velocity, pressure or full Navier–Stokes residual.

### 2.2 Impossibility for a source-only action

Assume $0<r\le1/4$ and $0<v\le1/8$. Define

$$z_+=1+v,\quad z_-=1-v,\qquad p_\pm=z_\pm^2+r.$$

An action observing only the same $r$ chooses the same scalar $g$. It then has residuals

$$E_\pm=g^2+2g-r\pm2vg.$$

If $E_+=E_-=0$, subtraction gives $4vg=0$. As $v>0$, $g=0$, which gives $E_\pm=-r\ne0$, a contradiction. What is equal is the declared summary $r$, not the targets $p_\pm$, the actual states or the full causal conditions. The choice of a deterministic source-only action is a stated restriction, not a description inferred from model outputs.

### 2.3 Exact minimax value

For any real numbers $A,B$, $\max(|A+B|,|A-B|)=|A|+|B|$. Hence

$$F(g):=\max(|E_+|,|E_-|)=|g^2+2g-r|+2v|g|.$$

Put $g_0=\sqrt{1+r}-1>0$. For $g\ge0$, the quadratic term changes sign only at $g_0$. For $0<g<g_0$,

$$F'(g)=-2g-2+2v<0,$$

and for $g>g_0$,

$$F'(g)=2g+2+2v>0.$$

Thus $F$ is minimised on that half-line at $g_0$, with value $2vg_0$. For $g\in[-2,0]$, $g^2+2g\le0$, so $F(g)\ge r$. Since $r=g_0(g_0+2)>2vg_0$, this interval cannot improve the minimum. For $g\le-2$, write $h=-g-2\ge0$. Then $g^2+2g=h^2+2h$ and

$$F(g)=|h^2+2h-r|+2vh+4v=F(h)+4v.$$

Consequently

$$\inf_{g\in\mathbb R}F(g)=2v(\sqrt{1+r}-1).$$

This piecewise proof establishes the whole stated range. The accompanying deterministic curve illustrates the analytical result; it is not its proof. This is elementary analysis applied to a specific continuation problem, not a claim of priority for minimax reasoning.

### 2.4 Why the one-variable repair can miss a leading term

Consider the earlier restricted interface

$$g=r/2+\chi(r)r^2B(r),$$

where $B$ is fixed and smooth near zero and $\chi=1$ there. Throughout this note and Note 9, asymptotic statements are as $\tau\to0$ for fixed real coefficients $a,b$ and fixed displayed coefficient functions and cutoffs. Implied constants may depend on these data; no uniformity over $a,b$ or a class of programmes is claimed. If $u=a\tau+O(\tau^2)$ and $r=b\tau^2+O(\tau^3)$, then $r^2B=O(\tau^4)$, while the leading complete residual is

$$E=ab\tau^3+O(\tau^4).$$

The state-dependent cross term is therefore not generally removable by that fixed interface. This does not exclude $\tau$-dependent families, finite interpolation, other algorithms or richer state representations. It does not retroactively classify an earlier finite model answer as incorrect.

### 2.5 A compact sufficient repair

Let $\chi_r$ and $\chi_u$ be fixed smooth cutoffs, with inner radii $1/4$ and $1/8$, and outer radii $1/2$ and $1/4$, respectively. Let $\Phi=\chi_r\chi_u$ and

$$g=r/2+\Phi[r^2B(r,u)+urC(r,u)].$$

On the closed outer rectangle, $z=1+u\ge3/4$ and $z^2+r\ge1/16$. Set $w=\sqrt{z^2+r}$ and

$$C_*=-\frac1{2z},\qquad B_*=-\frac1{2z(w+z)^2}.$$

These functions extend analytically to a neighbourhood of the rectangle. Rationalisation gives

$$w-z=\frac r{w+z}=\frac r{2z}-\frac{r^2}{2z(w+z)^2},$$

and $r/(2z)=r/2-ur/(2z)$. Thus where $\Phi=1$, $g=w-z$ and $E=0$. This formula supplies an actual sufficient alternative to a source-only action. The compact pair $(r,u)$ and the stated rule suffice for the local operation; an entire exploratory transcript is not mathematically necessary.

For general fixed smooth $B,C$ in this family, let $h=r^2B+urC$. Then

$$E=ur+r^2/4+\Phi(2+2u+r)h+\Phi^2h^2.$$

Under the scaling above,

$$E=ab[1+2C(0,0)]\tau^3+O(\tau^4).$$

This is a conditional prediction about an explicit rule, not a claim that the experimental model learned $C(0,0)=-1/2$ or that the later `hclose` task tests this coefficient.

### 2.6 Boundaries that must remain

The state-aware core formula does not assert an improvement in every norm outside that core. The earlier counterexample $z=9/10$, $r=1/5$ gives complete residual $-1/100$ for $g=r/2$ and $1/5-\sqrt{30}/25$ for $g=\sqrt{1+r}-1$. The latter has zero pure residual but a larger complete absolute residual. This is an analytical counterexample already present in the supplied history, not a newly run experiment.

The source weighted extension additionally assumes fixed-stage derivative bounds, smooth cutoffs, permissible slow-variable multipliers and flat edge weights. The constants may depend on the actual chosen functions. Neither finite evaluation nor a local analytic-domain certificate validates every full-PDE condition. Earlier source-relative arguments for full-cycle compatibility are preserved as those arguments, not upgraded to external independent review in this manuscript.

Likewise, changing a legitimate representation need not change its physical action. Under a compatible positive slow rescaling of the wave basis,

$$b_i'=\lambda_i b_i,\quad H'=H\operatorname{diag}(\lambda^2),\quad y'=y/\lambda^2,\quad d'=d/\lambda^2,$$

the relative source $r=d/y$ is unchanged. For a represented component $a_i b_i$, set $a_i'=a_i/\lambda_i$ with $\lambda_i>0$; then $a_i'b_i'=a_i b_i$. With $y_i=a_i^2$, the displayed transformation gives $H'y'=Hy$ and $d_i'/y_i'=d_i/y_i$. The required multiplier and inverse-derivative bounds are part of the statement. Arbitrary fast or unbounded rescaling is not licensed by algebra alone.

## Supplementary Note 3. Sufficient continuation

### 3.1 A declared future regime

Write $\mathcal F$ for a declared set of future duties, tools, permissions, resource rules and admissible continuation policies. Let $M(x)$ be the present output under comparison and $C(x)$ a candidate compact continuation state. Product equality $M(x)=M(x')$ does not by definition impose equality of $C$ or of future trajectories. Conversely, complete causal-state equality with identical transition and randomness laws gives the same future law. “History” explains a present difference only through a carrier or a boundary condition included in the model.

The theoretical use of “scientific future” here is deliberately conditional. It can include a new verification duty, a changed assumption, an opportunity to formulate a problem, or a method-reconstruction task. Equality on one finite set of tests does not prove equality on all future science. In particular, psychological understanding and the quality of generated scientific questions require their own operationalisations; they are not read out by the current compiler.

### 3.2 Conditional sufficiency proposition

Fix common standard Borel spaces for inputs, actions, observations, costs and compact states, and a finite horizon covered by $\mathcal F$. Suppose that (i) conditional on the compact state and policy-visible history, both systems use the same measurable exogenous-input kernel and policy kernel; (ii) conditional on the compact state, visible history, input and action, both use the same measurable joint kernel for the next observation, cost and compact state, with no remaining dependence on the hidden state $x$. Assume equal initial compact states and equal initial policy-visible histories. Alternatively, initial visible histories may differ if every kernel above factors through the compact state, so no residual dependence on those histories remains. Then the systems have the same joint law of future inputs, actions, observations, costs and compact successor states over that horizon.

**Proof.** The initial compact state and visible history coincide, or all kernels are independent of the potentially different prior histories. At the first step, integrating the common input kernel, policy kernel and joint observation-cost-successor kernel gives the same joint law. Conditional on each resulting finite history and compact state, the same kernels determine the next joint extension. Induction gives equality of the joint laws at every step up to the fixed horizon. In the alternative case, the same argument applies using the compact state alone. The statement is a standard sufficient-state composition principle, not a new result in probability or control theory.

The assumptions include the updates, not merely the prediction of the next answer. If duty changes reveal information not represented by $C$, the assumptions may cease to hold. If a decoder or a rederivation procedure reconstructs the required state but uses extra time or queries, outcome sufficiency may hold while budget-constrained equivalence does not. A claim of cost equivalence therefore includes construction, retrieval, compaction and restoration costs. Unobserved failure or missing events cannot be inserted as zero-cost steps.

### 3.3 Relation to a proof interface

If an existing result has type $L:\forall\theta,\ A(\theta)\to C(\theta)$ and a continuation consumes $I$, it is sufficient to preserve or reconstruct a valid parameter choice, a proof of its assumptions and a valid map from the resulting conclusion to $I$. Preserving the whole historical conversation is one possible carrier, not a logical requirement. The policy may also find another legitimate route to $I$. This statement describes an ordinary interface of proof composition; it does not explain by itself how that interface was learned, selected or socially allocated.

The mother question becomes empirical when those application relations, state records, checking responsibilities or reconstruction procedures are themselves outcomes of preceding activity. Their causal attribution requires real formation and use records, plus an appropriate comparison. The analytical witness specifies one dependency; actual coefficient programmes instantiate its whole-residual algebra in continuation records. The `hclose` case concerns a different application interface. No equality between their latent mechanisms is asserted.

### 9.3 First actual revision: preserving a method, changing its use

The following trace was selected after inspection as an explanatory example, not a confirmatory unit. Source identifiers are assistance P00, first continuation slot 0 and its actual successor. The existing programme uses

$$B_0=-\frac{1}{2(1+\sqrt{1+r})^2},\qquad C_0=0.$$

$$C_1=-\frac{1}{s(1+s)}.$$

$$E_1=-\frac{u^2r}{1+r}.$$
